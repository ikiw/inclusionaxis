Publications

White papers and research

  • White paper

    Beyond Reservation

    Reserved seats get disabled students into higher education. They do not, on their own, get them through it. This paper looks at what happens after admission — teaching, assessment, campus design and attitudes — and sets out what institutions need to change.

    Read Beyond Reservation PDF on Google Drive (opens in a new tab)

  • Research paper

    Women with disabilities in education

    Gender and disability compound each other. This paper examines where that happens in Indian education — enrolment, retention, safety, and the transition to work — and who is excluded twice as a result.

    Read the paper folder on Google Drive (opens in a new tab)
