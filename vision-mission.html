Vision & mission

Where we are going, and how

A society in which disabled people are expected, planned for and welcomed — in every classroom and every workplace.

Our vision

Inclusion as the default

An India where no student has to prove they belong before they are allowed to learn.

We want a country where accessibility is designed in from the start, where disabled people lead the decisions that affect them, and where the word “special” is no longer needed to describe an ordinary education.

Our mission

Five commitments

  • Raise awareness

    Bring disability rights into everyday conversation, in language people actually use.

  • Equip educators

    Give teachers practical, tested methods for teaching mixed-ability classrooms.

  • Shape policy

    Translate lived experience and evidence into recommendations institutions can adopt.

  • Open workplaces

    Move employers from token hiring to accessible, long-term careers.

  • Build coalitions

    Connect the people and organisations who can make the change permanent.

Our approach

Start with mindset, finish with design

Infrastructure without attitude fails. Attitude without infrastructure exhausts people. We work on both.

1. Listen first

Every engagement starts with disabled students, staff or employees describing what actually gets in their way.

2. Change the expectation

Awareness sessions and dialogues shift what an institution assumes about who its people are.

3. Build the capability

Training gives educators and managers concrete methods, not slogans.

4. Fix the system

Research and policy work change admissions rules, hiring processes and physical design so the improvement survives staff turnover.

In short

Our vision: disabled people are expected everywhere, so nobody has to ask for permission to take part.

Our mission: raise awareness, train educators, change policy, open workplaces, and build a coalition that keeps going.

How we work: we listen to disabled people first, then change what people believe, then change what they can do, then change the rules.
